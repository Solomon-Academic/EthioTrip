Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tour?
You can book a tour directly through our website by clicking the "Book Now" button on any destination or package page. Select your dates, number of travelers, and complete the payment process. You'll receive a confirmation email with your booking details.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 30+ days before travel receive a full refund. 15-29 days receive 50% refund. Less than 14 days receive no refund. However, you can transfer your booking to another person free of charge.
Do I need a visa to visit Ethiopia?
Most nationalities can get a visa on arrival or apply for an e-visa online. We recommend checking with your local Ethiopian embassy for specific requirements based on your country of origin.
What is the best time to visit Ethiopia?
The dry seasons (October-January and April-June) are ideal for most destinations. However, different regions have varying climates. Our travel consultants can help you choose the best time based on your preferred destinations.
Are your tours guided?
Yes, all our tours include professional, English-speaking local guides who are experts in Ethiopian history, culture, and wildlife. Multi-lingual guides can be arranged upon request.
